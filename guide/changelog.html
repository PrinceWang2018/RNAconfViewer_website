Package 0.7.6 — 2026-11-21 — RCV · RNA Conformation Viewer (engine rnafold3d 2.5.0)

Modified nucleotides in the prediction from sequence: 2D and 3D structures of RNA with m6A, pseudouridine, A-to-I editing, oxidative damage, 2′-O-methyl, LNA, phosphorothioate and more (docs/RCV.md §3.3). Z-RNA: left-handed helices marked in every displayed frame, and optionally predicted (§3.4). An audit of the basic functions (2026-11-20): cancelling stops the whole computation, more PDB entries open, ligands, ions and partner chains are shown next to the RNA, the 2D structure can be exported as dot-bracket, CT or BPSEQ, and errors are reported in words.

Added

  • Catalogue of 97 modified nucleotides (src/rnafold3d/modifications.py): methylations (m6A, m1A, m5C, m7G, m2,2G …), pseudouridine and m1Ψ, dihydrouridine, A-to-I (inosine) and C-to-U editing, damage (8-oxo-G, 8-oxo-A, O6-methyl-G), thio and halogen bases, 27 hypermodified tRNA nucleosides (t6A, i6A, queuosine, wybutosine, mnm5s2U …), sugar and backbone modifications (Nm, 2′-F, LNA, deoxy, phosphorothioate) and synthetic analogues (2-aminopurine, 2,6-diaminopurine, 5-bromo-U …). Each has its MODOMICS short name, aliases, PDB chemical component, parent base, effect on pairing, the atoms to build and a note.
  • Sequence notation: GGC[m6A]CU[Y]A*G (brackets for a modified nucleotide, * for a phosphorothioate), in the start page, FASTA files, batches and on the command line; rnafold3d predict --modifications POS:CODE,...; rnafold3d modifications [--json] lists the catalogue.
  • 2D with modifications: ViennaRNA's measured parameters (m6A, Ψ, inosine, dihydrouridine, purine, 7-deaza-A); generated soft constraints with estimated corrections for the others, including new pairs that only the modification allows (8-oxo-G·A, m2,2G·A, k2C·A); hard constraints keeping Watson-Crick-blocking modifications unpaired. They work together with probing data and alignments, and in evaluate.
  • 3D with modifications, inside the search (fold.py, loops.py, search.py):
  • sugar pucker preferences in the loop conformers drawn for non-helical residues (D and dN → C2′-endo; Nm, 2′-F, s2U, Ψ → C3′-endo; LNA locked C3′-endo); helical residues keep A-form;
  • syn/anti: a chi prior with a syn well for 8-oxo-G, 8-oxo-A and m8A (1.5–3 kT) and an anti bias for LNA and 2′-F, used by the annealing, the loop-closure torsion energy and the chi refinement;
  • modification atoms as riders of the model, placed from their base, sugar or phosphate frame before every contact evaluation and scored with the other atoms; removed atoms (O2′ of dN, N2 of I, O2 of s2U) left out;
  • interactions of the modification itself (modinteract.py, score term modification): hydrogen bonds of the polar groups it adds and of the donors it creates (Ψ N1-H, 8-oxo N7-H), with parent-atom roles updated for methylation; the Ψ N1-H water bridge to its own or the preceding phosphate (not for m1Ψ); a stacking change for residues outside helices (Ψ, m1Ψ, m5C, m6A, i6A/t6A, wybutosine, 5-halo-U stronger; D weaker);
  • a local relaxation of the modified residues and their neighbourhood at full steric weight;
  • the finished models get the final atoms (rotamers against the whole model); the manifest reports per modified residue its pucker and syn/anti against the preference, and the contacts before and after relaxation. Pairs a modification allows become helix restraints. PDB output writes HETATM and MODRES records, mmCIF the one-letter sequence with (CCD) and a _chem_comp loop with the parent. Unmodified sequences sample exactly as before.
  • Start page ▸ Ψ Modified nucleotides…: an editor window — clickable sequence map, searchable list per base with pairing badges and notes, phosphorothioate linkages, presets (m6A at DRACH motifs, every U → m1Ψ / Ψ) and a summary table. The status line counts the modifications.
  • Viewer: Colour by ▸ Modified nucleotides with a legend by kind, the modification in the hover tip, select modified and resn m6A in the console, a paragraph in the methods text; modified residues of opened PDB/mmCIF files are recognised by their PDB component.
  • AlphaFold 3 (local) passes modifications with a PDB component in its input JSON.
  • GET /api/modifications, POST /api/modifications/parse; tests/test_v076.py.
  • Z-RNA identification (src/rnafold3d/zrna.py, src/rcv/static/js/zrna.js), on by default: regions of at least three stacked Watson-Crick pairs with left-handed two-step twist and syn purines / anti pyrimidines, found on every displayed frame (conformations, morphs, scrubbing, video, pulling) and drawn with their own theme — halo, dashed axis and badge in 3D, halo and tag in 2D, also in image, panel and video exports. View ▸ Z-RNA regions switches it; select zrna in the console; zrna_regions per model in the manifest. Finds the Z duplexes of PDB 2GXB and 1T4X (12/12 models) and nothing in the 3964 chains of the template library.
  • Z-RNA prediction (Advanced settings ▸ Predict Z-RNA (left-handed helices) when likely, off by default; rnafold3d predict --zrna auto --zrna-salt M --zrna-binder): an estimated A→Z free energy per helix segment (dinucleotide steps, salt, junctions, 8-substituted purines, Z-binding protein); below +3 kcal/mol a hypothesis with that segment built as a Z-form stem (build_stem(..., form='Z'), torsions fitted to 1T4X and 2GXB, 1.1–1.4 Å from them) and the junction pairs opened, with a quarter more trajectories. The estimate and the candidates are in the manifest (input.zrna) and the methods text.
  • m8G and 8-Br-G in the modification catalogue (syn guanosines; Z-RNA promoters).
  • tests/test_zrna.py, tests/js/zrna_unit.mjs (Python and JavaScript detectors give the same regions), tests/data/zrna_2gxb_duplex.pdb; smoke scenarios zrna, zrnashot.

Submission steps: packaging/README.md.
  • RCV.exe carries a Windows version resource (publisher, product, description, version).
  • What is bound to the RNA is shown (audit C5; rcv/data.py context_of, static/js/viewer/context.js, contextctl.js). Opening a PDB entry kept the RNA chain and dropped everything else, so a riboswitch appeared without its ligand and an RNA–protein complex as bare RNA. Now ligands are drawn as sticks, ions as spheres and protein, DNA and other RNA chains as thin traces (one point per residue), named on hover. They move with their conformation (superposition, morphs, scrubbing, mechanism playback, figure panels, videos), are stored in .rcv files and shown on the interactive HTML page. AlphaFold 3 results with partner chains show them the same way. Smoke scenarios bound, boundshot.
  • Export of the secondary structure (requested): Export ▸ Secondary structure (dot-bracket, CT, BPSEQ)…, the download button of the 2D window, and rcv ss INPUT [-o FILE] [--format dbn|ct|bpseq] [--which distinct|all] [--conformation N]. Changed

  • Sequences copied from a database record can be pasted as they are (audit C1): position numbers, spaces and a closing // are ignored (1 ggcagatctg agcctgggag …), in the start page, FASTA files, batches and rnafold3d predict --sequence. Digits inside the brackets of a modified nucleotide ([m2,2G]) are kept.
  • A known 2D structure is checked before the run (audit C2): length, unmatched and unclosed brackets with their position, characters that are not dot-bracket, and hairpin loops shorter than 3 nt (which made every trajectory fail after minutes). The same check in the start page, the batch list, the server and the engine.
  • Cancel (audit C3): a run older than a minute asks first, because what was computed is discarded and a cancelled run cannot be continued; the button shows Cancelling… until the engine has stopped.
  • Notes of a running prediction can be read (audit C4): the list follows the newest note only while it is scrolled to the bottom; it is refilled only when a note arrives, so a selection in it survives.
  • Start page wording (audit C6): the empty box no longer says twice that the demo sequence will be used.
  • The window says when something is missing or breaks (audit B1–B3, B5): a lost connection to the engine is stated in the progress card, which keeps the last known state and reconnects (before: an empty card after one failed request); without WebGL 2 a page explains the usual reasons instead of a blank window; a lost graphics context is announced and the view redrawn when it is back; errors that no code handles are shown in a note and written to the log. In browser-window mode RCV quits ten minutes after its window is gone, unless a computation is running; a computer that slept does not count.
  • Local server (audit B6): the session token is checked before the body of a request is read; a body that is not a JSON object, and numbers given as words, are answered with status 400 and a sentence naming the value.
  • Messages for files that cannot be read (audit B4): a truncated ATOM line, a coordinate that is not a number and an incomplete mmCIF atom table are reported with the file, the line or atom and what is wrong; a file without RNA says what it holds (“protein (129 residues) and 2 ligands or ions (MG, SAM)”). The engine's command line reports impossible values (--models 0, --seed -1, --modifications abc:m6A) in one line.
  • Mirror-image RNA (L-nucleotides) opens with a note that the analyses are made for natural D-RNA, and is left out when the template library is rebuilt.
  • CASP16 result files, figure and tables regenerated from the 0.7.6 runs (benchmark/casp16/results/).
  • Engine 2.5.0: the fit onto the template is repeated instead of given up (predict.py, PredictConfig.template_fit_attempts, default 4). The last stage of a template model moves every atom onto the template. When the result failed the geometry checks, the fit was discarded and the model kept the looser shape of the torsion-space search — in about half of the template models. Now the residues with the problems and their neighbours (one more on each side per attempt) are released from the template and the fit is repeated, up to 35% of the chain; models whose fit is still rejected rank after those with an accepted fit. Measured with the step on and off, same seeds:
  • 48 library chains with a template, released after their templates (benchmark/template_models.py): rejected fits 73 of 150 → 8 of 138; mean TM-score of model 1 0.582 → 0.605 (+0.023, 95% interval +0.007 to +0.043; 8 chains better by more than 0.02, 2 worse), best of 5 0.613 → 0.622 (+0.009, −0.001 to +0.023).
  • CASP16, 15 targets (runs_v076 against runs_v076_fit1, which reproduces 0.7.5 exactly): model 1 0.495 → 0.501 (+0.006, −0.000 to +0.017; R1271 0.39 → 0.47, the others unchanged), best of 5 0.527 → 0.527; right global fold on 9 of 15 targets (8), median RMSD of model 1 7.8 → 6.0 Å; 68 minutes for the 15 targets with and without the step.
  • The six chains with a template of the 26-chain development set: model 1 0.508 → 0.497 (two chains −0.02 to −0.03; within the noise of six).
  • Five templates instead of three (max_templates=5, run fit4_t5): model 1 +0.0001, best of 5 +0.0003 on the 48 chains; three stay the default. Runs without a template are unchanged; template runs differ from 0.7.5 for the same seed. template_fit_attempts=1 gives the former behaviour.
  • Confidence model checked blind on CASP16 (benchmark/casp16/confidence_check.py, docs/V07_VALIDATION.md §F): colour bands in the right order, the high band somewhat optimistic; models fitted onto a distant template are over-rated (the model reads coordinates only). Stated in the manual (§8.5). A correction with the template's rank score and choosing model 1 by confidence were tested and not adopted. The model itself is unchanged.
  • Variants (Switch tab): one entry that cannot be read no longer stops the whole list; the others are analysed and the unusable ones are listed with the reason. Positions are the residue numbers RCV shows (a structure whose file starts at residue 17 was addressed by counting from 1 while the table showed the file's numbers). 34G>U and an n./r. prefix are read; a variant entered twice counts once.
  • Target sites (Details tab) about 100 times faster, and every window on long RNAs. The opening energy of all windows now comes from one pass over the ensemble (the unpaired probabilities of RNAplfold with window and pair span set to the full length) instead of one constrained partition function per window. The numbers are the same (difference below 0.0001 kcal/mol on RNAs of 76–3000 nt, at 37 °C/1 M and 25 °C/0.15 M Na⁺): 300 nt take 0.2 s instead of 20 s, 1500 nt 15 s instead of about 40 min. RNAs longer than 400 nt are no longer sampled at every n-th window. The former method remains as a fallback for a ViennaRNA without that interface.
  • The Pull tool stands out in the toolbar: accent-coloured, its label shown at every window width, and a small dot marks it until it is first used.
  • Base-pair annotation accepts a pair whose non-central hydrogen bond is missing only for three-bond pairs, so inosine–C (two hydrogen bonds) is annotated.
  • Pairs across a chain break (O3′–P not bonded) are annotated even when the residues are fewer than four apart, and the 2D energy of such a model is the co-folding energy of its strands. The RNA chain option accepts several chains joined, e.g. A+B, to show both strands of a duplex.

Package 0.7.5 — 2026-11-15 — RCV · RNA Conformation Viewer (engine rnafold3d 2.3.0)

First public release since 0.7.3: it also contains everything listed under 0.7.4, which was not released on its own.

Conformation-selective compound screening (docs/NEXT_STEPS.md §1), steps 1 to 5: where a compound could bind, how those sites differ between two conformations, a scoring function and a docking search for RNA, and the screen itself — a compound library ranked by how much better it fits one conformation than the other, in its own window and on the command line. It is marked experimental and shows what it was measured at, because one of its three benchmarks is still missing (docs/SCREENING_STATUS.md).

Added

  • Pocket finding (src/rnafold3d/screening/pockets.py, a new rnafold3d.screening package). A buriedness scan of the free space around the atoms on a 0.8 Å grid (the LIGSITE approach: a point counts as enclosed in a direction when the RNA closes the space on both sides within 12 Å, along the three axes and the four cube diagonals), clustered into sites of at least 100 Å³ enclosed on at least five of the seven directions. Each site has its volume, how enclosed it is, the nucleotides lining it, how much of that lining lies outside a Watson-Crick helix, and a score that ranks the sites (enclosed × outside-a-helix × size). find() takes plain coordinates and depends only on numpy and scipy; a 76-nt tRNA takes 0.2 s, a 158-nt structure 1 s.
  • benchmark/screening/pocket_sites.py: thirty experimental RNA-compound complexes with the compound removed; the site it occupied is the top-ranked pocket in 21 of 30 and among those found in 24. This is what set the threshold: at 6 of 7 enclosing directions it is 9 and 12, because most RNA sites are shallow.
  • volume_near() and compare(): the same site measured in two conformations — the open volume held by the nucleotides that line a pocket — so pockets that merge or split between the conformations are still compared, and the superposition of the two structures does not matter. A site open in one conformation and closed in the other is what a conformation-selective compound would use.
  • *Details tab ▸ Pockets: where a compound could bind*** (closed advanced section, as in 0.7.4): the pocket table of the shown conformation, Compare with a second conformation and the sites that differ between the two, Colour 3D by pocket, copy as a table. A row selects the nucleotides lining the pocket in 3D and 2D. only deep pockets (6 of 7) keeps the closed sites only. Manual: docs/RCV.md §8.6.
  • Server: POST /api/insight/pockets (conformation, other, burial_min); it does not need ViennaRNA.
  • Tests: tests/test_v075.py (15 tests, among them the cavity of a hollow sphere against its analytic volume); GUI smoke test: the insightpockets scenario (62 in total).

From pictures of conformations to the mechanism between them: how A turns into B, how fast, through which intermediates, which base pairs and which coordinated 3D motions limit the switch, and which mutations, salt or ligand change it. Added

  • Pathway tab: transition from conformation A to B (rcv/transition.py, js/panels/pathway.js; user guide 8.1).
  • Basins: A and B first relax into their local minima of the 2D landscape (steepest descent), and kinetics are measured between the basins. A and B in one basin are reported as fluctuations of one fold.
  • Routes: the direct path between the basins (ViennaRNA findpath), plus a network of intermediates: the basins of the ensemble's other 2D states and local minima from Boltzmann samples (fixed seed, reproducible).
  • Kinetics: transition path theory gives committors and the routes ranked by reactive flux. The mean first-passage time gives the switching time, the half-life of A and the effective barrier; k₀ is adjustable.
  • Traps: wells deeper than A that hold the RNA for most of the switching time, found from the expected time spent in each node.
  • Display: a plain-language verdict, the routes on the ensemble map, and the energy profile (relaxation, route, direct path, saddle) with hover showing the base pairs that open or form.
  • Bottleneck and coordinated motions: the cooperative unit (base pairs that open together on the climb to the highest saddle) and the nucleus of B. In 3D, rigid modules (helix-seeded, merged within 2 Å), with their turn, shift, screw axis and time window, plus hinges. The module that carries the unit is named the rate-limiting coordinated motion. Colour by mechanism, event order or module.

  • Topology-aware ensemble map (rcv/landscape.py, js/landscape.js; Energy tab ▸ Map, also in the Pathway tab, the plot studio and the HTML page):
